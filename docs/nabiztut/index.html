NabızTut

Is it up? Just glance.

A desktop uptime monitor for your websites, APIs and servers. Turkish for “keep the pulse”.

Download for Mac View source

v1.0.0 · macOS 14 or later · Apple silicon · English & Turkish

Your sites' pulse, always in sight

Desktop panel

Sits on your desktop, below your windows. Status, response time, uptime and a sparkline for every site.

HTTP(S) checks

Expected status codes, keyword in the response, custom method, headers and body, redirect handling.

Ping & TCP

Watch servers, databases and any service port, not just websites.

Down alerts

Notification and sound when a site goes down, another when it recovers with the outage duration.

Charts & history

24 h, 7 d and 30 d response-time charts with outages marked, uptime %, average and p95.

SSL expiry warning

A one-time heads-up before a certificate expires, so nothing breaks on a Sunday.

Secrets in Keychain

API keys and tokens in headers are stored in the macOS Keychain, not in a plain settings file.

100% local

No server, no account, no tracking. Your list of sites stays on your Mac.

Polite by design

Requests

  • Check interval1 – 60 min · default 5
  • Checks are spread outnever all at once
  • Marked down after2 failures · 30 s apart
  • Page content not neededHEAD
  • Identifies itselfUser-Agent: NabizTut

Data & sleep

  • Every checkkept 7 days
  • Hourly summarieskept 30 days
  • Mac asleep or offlinechecks pause
  • Sleep timenever counted as downtime
  • Stored in~/Library/Application Support

Install

  1. Download NabizTut.zip, open it and move NabizTut.app to your Applications folder.
  2. NabızTut isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS blocks the first launch. Try to open it once, then go to System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
  3. Allow notifications, add your first site and drag the panel by its top bar to where you want it.

Build from source

Requires Xcode or the Command Line Tools.

git clone https://github.com/mrtozn1923/macos-apps.git
cd macos-apps/NabizTut
./scripts/build.sh --install

The app's interface follows your macOS language: Turkish or English.
